Board ops calendar

Private ops reminder for Jamie — daily community board skim. Not a product feature; not public marketing.

What you get

A daily calendar event at 9:00 AM Central (15 minutes), with alerts at the start and 10 minutes before. Notes point at one command: python3 tools/moderate.py check — that is your reports inbox (not in the iPhone app).

Subscribe on iPhone / Mac Download .ics

If the button does nothing

  1. Open the .ics link in Safari.
  2. Or: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar
  3. Server: https://riseandpray.app/ops/board-ops.ics

After subscribe, open the event once and confirm an alert is set (some devices ignore feed alarms).

Change the time

Edit the ICS in the riseandpray-legal repo (ops/board-ops.ics), push, then wait for Calendar to refresh the subscription (or remove and re-add).
